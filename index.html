courses

1.CATERING

Catering skills include culinary expertise (menu planning, cooking, presentation), strong customer service and communication abilities, and logistical and organizational skills for event planning and execution. Key abilities also involve meticulous attention to detail for accuracy and cleanliness, adaptability to client needs, and the capacity to work effectively under pressure.

2.CHEF

Chef skills include both technical abilities like cooking techniques, knife skills, and menu development, and soft skills like leadership, creativity, time management, and communication. They must also possess qualities such as stamina, attention to detail, and the ability to work effectively under pressure in a fast-paced environment.

3.CULINARY SKILLS

Culinary skills include a wide range of abilities from knife handling and heat control to flavor balancing, recipe development, and professional presentation. These skills are essential for safely and effectively preparing and presenting food, ranging from basic techniques like chopping and simmering to advanced methods like creating sauces and understanding molecular gastronomy. A comprehensive description involves both the technical and creative aspects of cooking, along with professional practices like kitchen sanitation and food safety.

4.WAITERS

Waiter skills include excellent customer service and communication, physical stamina and the ability to work under pressure, strong multitasking and organizational abilities, and attention to detail. They must also be able to handle point-of-sale systems, process payments, and manage food safety standards.
